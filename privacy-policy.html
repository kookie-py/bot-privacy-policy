Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord application ("the Bot") handles information when it is used in Discord servers.

Information We Access

The Bot may receive information provided by Discord through the Discord API that is necessary for its functionality. This may include Discord user IDs, server membership information, usernames, display names, nicknames, avatars, and other member information made available through Discord's API.

How We Use Information

The Bot uses this information only to provide and operate its support ticket and security features.

In particular, the Bot may detect changes to a member's display name, nickname, or avatar while they are associated with a support ticket. This is used to alert support staff about potential impersonation attempts and help them verify the identity of users participating in a ticket.

The Bot does not use this information for advertising, selling data, profiling users, or tracking users for unrelated purposes.

Data Storage

The Bot does not intentionally store Discord member profile information or member update history for unrelated purposes.

Information required to operate the ticket system may be stored, such as Discord user IDs, ticket IDs, channel IDs, and ticket status.

Information is retained only for as long as it is necessary to operate the Bot's stated functionality or as otherwise required by applicable law.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share Discord API Data with advertisers, data brokers, or other unrelated third parties.

If a third-party service is used to operate part of the Bot's infrastructure, access to data is limited to what is necessary to provide that service and is subject to applicable privacy and security requirements.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of personal information stored by the Bot by contacting the Bot's operator through the support/contact method provided with the Bot.

Where the information is no longer necessary for the Bot's functionality and there is no legal requirement to retain it, it will be deleted.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by the Bot against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's functionality or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version of this policy will be made publicly available at this location.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests concerning the Bot, please contact the Bot's operator through the support channel in our Discord server https://discord.gg/jacemarket. You may also contact us by email at kookiejung310@gmail.com.
